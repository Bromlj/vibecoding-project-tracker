30-second pitch example

StudySprint helps students plan study sessions. Our fictional team is building an accessible timer, saved notes and weekly goals. In the tracker, each teammate can see tasks, review failed approaches and carry useful lessons into the next AI briefing.

Team walkthrough

  1. Open “StudySprint · team example”.
  2. Look at the three fictional teammates and their assigned tasks.
  3. Open “Build accessible study timer”, then Attempts & lessons.
  4. Read three approaches; choose which ones to include and review the exact briefing.
  5. Use Details to hand off to another member. Save and check that the owner badge changes.
  6. View project milestones, then try Appearance & settings and Accessibility.

The team example runs locally. It does not connect another person or create an authenticated account.

Example evaluation notes

Problem: counting interval callbacks can drift when a tab is inactive. Proposed decision: calculate remaining time from an end timestamp. Accessibility decision: announce meaningful timer changes rather than every second. Next evaluation: observe keyboard and screen-reader users completing a study session.

These are example reasoning notes, not completed research results.

Example project documentation

Team: Alex implements goals, Sam investigates defects, Jordan reviews accessibility. Tasks can be reassigned. A milestone is reached only when its stated criterion has been checked.

Attempt records keep the prompt, tool, result, lesson and author label. Checking “include in briefing” selects material for the next handover. Saving a task persists edits; copying alone does not save or call an AI service.
