The lecture methods help choose and evaluate the interface. They are not a requirement to put questionnaires, card-sorting tools, SDDP workshops or statistical dashboards inside this Kanban application. The student's own final explanation should use the actual sources, decisions and observations they can defend.

Latest colour/appearance revision: stronger whole-card semantic colours and three alternative palettes respond to direct user feedback. Labels, icons and distinct border styles support colour-independent interpretation. Light/Dark/System is a browser preference with older-record compatibility; checks and the superseded neutral-Done decision are documented in DESIGN.md and RELEASE_READINESS.md.
